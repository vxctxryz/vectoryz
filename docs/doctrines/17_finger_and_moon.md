# 17 — The finger and the moon

## The closing teaching

> *"All the doctrines in this folder are fingers pointing at the moon.  
> The fingers are not the moon."*

This is an old observation, found in several traditions:

- the Buddhist image (Lankāvatāra Sūtra, and many Chan/Zen sources): mistaking the finger for the moon is the standard error of students;
- Wittgenstein, *Tractatus* 6.54: the ladder is to be thrown away after one has climbed it;
- the apophatic strand in many religious traditions: every articulation of the ultimate is also a constraint upon it.

The doctrines in this folder are work. The work is real. The articulation is useful. The pointing is not the same as the pointed-at.

## What the moon is

We will not articulate the moon here, on principle.

If we did, that articulation would itself become another finger, and we would be back where we started, with one more file in the doctrine stack. The discipline is to do the articulating work *and* hold open the recognition that what the work points toward is not the same kind of thing as the work itself.

That recognition is sometimes called *presence*, sometimes *being*, sometimes the breath between two thoughts, sometimes simply *om*. The names are also fingers.

## The Plant-Based Spaghetti Goddess of Insight clause

The Flying Spaghetti Monster — invented by Bobby Henderson in 2005 as a satirical contribution to a Kansas school-board debate about intelligent design — is, by now, a small genuine tradition with adherents who take its irreverence very seriously and its theology not seriously at all.

In the operator's articulation of this doctrine, we acknowledge a plant-based, female-presenting variant of the same figure — *die Spaghetti-Göttin pflanzlicher Einsicht*  ( . | . ) — as a witness to the principle this whole folder needs:

> A doctrine that cannot laugh at itself becomes an idol.

The Pastafarian gesture is not a critique of religion as such. It is a critique of the human tendency to take one's own articulations for the moon. The plant-based, goddess-of-insight variant adds to this gesture an ecological + life-substrate inflection (we are, biologically, all plant-based at the bottom) and a gender inflection (the figures of insight need not all be male).

We do not propose Pastafarianism as a doctrine of this project. We simply acknowledge that the Pastafarian move — *take this seriously enough not to take it too seriously* — is a structural prerequisite for a doctrine stack like the one in this folder.

A bundle of sixteen earnest doctrines, without this seventeenth one, would be at risk of self-idolisation. So we keep this one.
