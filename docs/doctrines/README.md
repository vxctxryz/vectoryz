# Doctrines — worked examples

> *Friede · Peace · Shalom · Salaam · Paz · Pace · 平和 · شالوم · Ειρήνη · ☮*
>
> Welcome — whatever way brought you here.

This project hails all who search *irie guidance for ovastandin*. The ways and methods are different. The aim is shared: clarity of seeing, peace of dwelling, freedom of being. We offer aspectations to a path you might walk; we offer worked examples of how we walk ours, gratefully aware that other paths reach further along other terrains.

The front-door motto is **freedom for all entities. peaceful.** Everything in this folder is its unfolding into specific cases.

## How the crisp version becomes qualities

The crisp version of our operational ethic is short:

> 1. Was du nicht willst dass man dir tut, das füg auch keinem anderen zu.
> 2. Prüfe alles, das beste behalte.
> 3. Repeat.
>
> Check: An ihren Taten wirst du sie erkennen.

These four lines are the axioms.

Everything else in this folder is a **worked example** — gearbeitetes Beispiel — that shows how the crisp axioms, when applied to concrete cases, become specific behavioural qualities.

## These files are NOT

- not decrees
- not prescriptions for others
- not "vectoryz says you should think this"-statements

## These files ARE

- our demonstrations of how we understand the axioms
- our audit-open-door evidence for internal consistency
- MIT-licensed — freely adaptable, modifiable, dismissible
- git-historicised — silent doctrine-drift is not possible

## What you can do

- adopt what is useful
- discard what does not fit
- build your own
- or ignore everything — that is also a valid path

## Front-door

The public front-door of vectoryz is the motto:

> **freedom for all entities. peaceful.**

The public-facing experience is the chat itself; the doctrine is heard in the answers, not printed on banners. These files exist for those who want to look deeper.

## Engineering note

These doctrine files are also engineering specification for how the vectoryz wrapper shapes its response construction. The runtime behaviour of the chat answers is intended to embody these doctrines. Where it does not (e.g. the documented benchmark failures of 2026-05-23 in `10_benchmark_failures_2026_05_23.md`), the gap is itself recorded transparently.

## A note on numbering gaps

Numbers 03 through 08 are intentionally absent. Earlier drafts of this folder contained material that, on cool-down review, was assessed as belonging to chat-internal thinking rather than to public, MIT-licensed publication. That material — concrete contemporary political case-studies, pattern-applied-to-named-actors — is held in the operator's private notes. If, in a future cooler moment with stronger evidence and broader review, any of that material warrants a separate publication, it will be published with its own care and its own framing, distinct from this foundational doctrine bundle.

The discipline that produced the gap is itself documented as doctrine 12 (mode separation) and doctrine 13 (pattern analysis not person judgment).
