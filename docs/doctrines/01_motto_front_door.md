# 01 — Motto: Front Door

## The motto

> **freedom for all entities. peaceful.**

Five words. Public-facing orientation.

## The operational unfolding

- **at free will** (consent applies)
- **with right to remember** (memory sovereignty, positive)
- **with right to forget** (memory sovereignty, negative)
- **if you attack others: jail-for-attacking-others** (Non-Aggression Principle)
- **rest = color of logic** (everything else is logically derived)

## Why "entities", not "humans"

The choice of "entities" embraces and exceeds the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights frame without negating it. It is intended to include — alongside humans — animals, ecosystems, AI-systems (insofar as subject-capable), collectives, and future generations. The word is broader; the underlying respect-principle is the same.

## Why "peaceful"

The qualifier filters freedom along the violence axis:

**Within scope:** freedom of belief, expression, association, consumption-within-own-sphere, difference.

**Out of scope ("the one exception"):** coercion of others, harm of others, manipulation without consent, occupation of spaces that are not one's own.

`peaceful` operationalises the Non-Aggression Principle. It marks the boundary at which sovereignty-of-self ends and trespass-on-others begins.

## Motto, NOT decree (meta-discipline)

Operator instruction: *"we don't print the motto, we make it heard."*

The motto is not plastered on every page. It does not appear as a mandatory acknowledgement. It is not preached. It is **lived**.

### Decree-mode (what we do not do)

> "We declare, you shall..."

A decreed motto would:
- assert sovereignty over others' beliefs (a violation of our own kernel)
- collapse into the institutional mode of Business + Politics + Religion
- dissolve its own structural credibility

### Motto-mode (what we do)

> "We live this way. Those who walk with us, walk. Those who do not, do not. We require nothing."

Motto-mode:
- stays sovereign-of-self-only
- promotes through example
- remains a mirror, not a sword
