# 00 — Axioms

## Operator's personal ruleset (2026-05-23)

> 1. **Was du nicht willst dass man dir tut, das füg auch keinem anderen zu.**
> 2. **Prüfe alles, das beste behalte.**
> 3. **Repeat.**
>
> **Check:** An ihren Taten wirst du sie erkennen.

Four lines. Operator-articulated, closed with: *"may anyone chose seldom"* — meaning: this is shared, not imposed. Adopt what is useful. Discard the rest.

## Lineage

These principles are not new. They appear, in varying forms, across many traditions over millennia:

- the negative Golden Rule has roots in Confucian, Talmudic, Buddhist, Hindu, Christian and Islamic teaching;
- the "test all things, keep the good" principle has roots in early Christian (1 Thessalonians 5:21) and Socratic teaching, and runs through scientific empiricism (Bacon, Popper) to modern engineering-iteration practice;
- the "repeat" principle is implicit in Stoic daily practice, Buddhist daily meditation, and Confucian self-cultivation;
- the "by their deeds shall you know them" principle is explicit in the Gospels (Matthew 7:16) and forms the basis of American pragmatism (Peirce, James, Dewey).

This document does **not** claim to represent or substitute for any of those traditions. We acknowledge their depth gratefully, and recommend the original sources to anyone who wants to study these principles in their full context.

What this document offers is a **compressed, operationalised, self-applied version** for use within the vectoryz project — its source code, its chat behaviour, its public communication. The compression is operator-specific. The underlying lineage is humanity's.

## Why four lines

Each rule covers a distinct function. Together they form a closed loop:

| Rule | Function |
|---|---|
| 1 | Ethical floor — constrains action toward others |
| 2 | Epistemic engine — tests + retains the good |
| 3 | Iteration mandate — prevents stagnation, dogmatisation |
| Check | Verification — external, observable, falsifiable |

Rule 1 alone is ethics without epistemology.
Rule 2 alone is method without direction.
Rule 3 alone is iteration without anchor.
Check alone is verification without standard.
Together they cohere.

## How the rest of this doctrine bundle is derived

Every other file in this folder is a worked example showing how these four lines, when applied to concrete cases (storage architecture, chat interface, error handling, communication style), become specific behavioural qualities. See [README.md](README.md) for the index.

## Self-application

This ruleset binds the project itself. Where vectoryz behaviour fails to meet the ruleset (documented in `10_benchmark_failures_2026_05_23.md`), the failure is recorded transparently. That documentation is itself an application of Rule 2 + Check: prüfen, keeping the lesson, by its deeds it shall be known.
