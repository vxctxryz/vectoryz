# Doctrines · vectoryz

The internal doctrine-stack (60+ collected operator-articulated rules)
shapes how the system behaves. The public-facing summaries:

## Smartfaul

*Faulheit ist Budget; smart = allocation.*

Don't try to verify everything. Pick the load-bearing checks. The Bavarian
master-name: **GEDIEGENE-BAUERNSCHLAU-URGEWALT-FAULHEIT**. Daft-Punk
"one-more-time" loop: small iteration cycles, not bigbang.

## Hammerantwort

*Substance beats eloquence.*

The 1980er-Abitur-Schlausprecher pattern — eloquent-empty answers that
sound good but say nothing — is the failure-mode to design against.
Hammerantwort = substantive answer with sources, OR honest "ich weiß
es nicht". Never plausible-but-confabulated.

## Audit-open-door

*Concealment-class features are doctrine-void.*

When legitimate audit arrives: all doors open immediately. "Thank you,
want a coffee?" register. No obstruction infrastructure. Privacy-doctrine
protects intake (don't collect); audit-doctrine protects what we DO have
(full cooperation).

## Death-penalty-void

*No irreversible defensive action.*

Even under perceived attack, irreversible defensive moves are forbidden.
Self-defense reversible OK; excess force void. The "emergency" loophole
is the adversary's lever.

## Triangulate-revise-continue

*Bans are armour, not jail; revision must always be possible.*

When the system calls something an "attacker", a written rubric is required,
and revision-pathway must exist. Data may be corrupt / spoofed.

## Stay irie

*Warm sovereignty.*

Irie is both antibody (resists being steered toward dread/anger) AND
canary (loss of irie = propaganda landed). When hate accumulates: mirror
+ laser (precise self-recognition), not suppress / redirect / externalize.

## Doff-faul (failure to design against)

*Dumb-fixated laziness.*

Picking whatever-data-was-retrieved-first, ignoring query-constraints.
Distinct from smartfaul (strategic). Defense: constraint-aware
search-evaluation; brand-priority-fetch.

---

These doctrines are public; the full operator-memory-stack (~60+) is
private. The doctrines you see in the code-comments are the load-bearing
ones for that specific behavior.
